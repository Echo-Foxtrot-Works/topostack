type: fix
title: Keep airspace acrylic within the supported stock range
---
Airspace acrylic now stays within its supported 1–10 mm range, including when its thickness follows the terrain material. Turning on **Airspace in 3D** with thinner or thicker terrain stock starts with 3 mm acrylic, which you can change in the Aviation panel. Changing terrain stock outside that range keeps the current acrylic thickness.

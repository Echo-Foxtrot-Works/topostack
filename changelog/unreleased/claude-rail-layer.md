---
type: feature
title: Add railways to your maps, with romanized names where the local script can't be engraved
---
Turn on **Railways** under Map features to engrave above-ground rail lines from OpenStreetMap, in their own SVG group so you can give them their own laser setting. Tunnels, yards and sidings are left out. Road and rail labels in scripts the engraving fonts can't draw, such as Japanese, now use the English or romanized name, so 東海道本線 engraves as "Tokaido Main Line" instead of disappearing.

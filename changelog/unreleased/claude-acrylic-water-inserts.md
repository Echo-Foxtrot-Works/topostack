---
type: feature
title: Cut lakes from acrylic
---
Turn on **Acrylic water inserts** in Fabrication settings and each lake is cut out of the sheet at its waterline and filled with a fitted acrylic piece, resting on a small ledge, with the lake bed showing through. The acrylic comes as its own files with its own kerf, fit clearance and sheet layout, map detail over the water is engraved on it, and the assembly guide shows where each insert goes. See [export files](/guides/export-files).

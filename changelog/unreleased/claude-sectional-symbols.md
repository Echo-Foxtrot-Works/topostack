type: improvement
title: Aviation symbols drawn as the VFR sectional legend draws them
---
Airports show fuel ticks, beacon stars, filled hard-surface discs, runway layouts, and the private, military and seaplane symbols. Navaids and obstacles use the chart's own shapes, including high-intensity lights, wind turbines and obstacle groups. Special use airspace is hatched on its inside edge. Where symbols crowd, private fields, heliports and shorter obstacles give way so nothing prints over anything else, and identifiers keep clear of every symbol. Military fields with a hard runway show their runway layout. Close parallel runways stay apart in a runway layout symbol.

---
type: feature
title: Sea floors now use GEBCO's global ocean depths, about four times sharper than before
---
Layered models and engravings that reach the sea now carve the sea floor from the GEBCO_2026 grid (about 460 m) instead of the coarse ocean in the base terrain, everywhere in the world. Trenches, canyons and shelves such as Suruga Bay and the Japan Trench now show their shape. Coastlines and land are unchanged, and inland models load exactly as before.

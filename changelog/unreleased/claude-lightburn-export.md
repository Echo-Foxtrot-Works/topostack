type: feature
title: Open your project in LightBurn with its layers already set up
---
**LightBurn projects** under Individual files in Export gives one `.lbrn2` per panel or nested sheet, plus the master, with engraving, assembly ids, score and cut each on a named layer in running order. Set speed and power, then run. A new [xTool Studio guide](/guides/xtool-studio) covers importing the SVG there. See the [LightBurn guide](/guides/lightburn).

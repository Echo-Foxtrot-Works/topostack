---
type: improvement
title: Shared links and saved projects open sooner
---
The studio now opens your saved project, or the design in a shared, example or lake link, before it draws the 3D preview. It used to draw the Crater Lake sample first and then replace it. On a slow computer, a shared link that generates on arrival finished its terrain in about 2 seconds instead of 4, and its design opened in under a second instead of nearly 4. If restoring takes longer than a second, for example while an example downloads, the sample preview shows in the meantime.

---
type: improvement
title: Layered coastlines now follow the mapped shore instead of the coarse elevation data
---
The sea-level sheet of a layered model is now cut along the OpenStreetMap coastline, so shores, islands and harbours line up with the engraved shoreline instead of stepping along the elevation data's coarse pixels. Reclaimed land that older elevation data still showed as sea, such as Hiroshima's port, is now land.

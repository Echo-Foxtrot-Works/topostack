---
type: improvement
title: Export is now a step-by-step flow
---
Exporting a layered project walks through one decision at a time: **Panels** for nests and seams, **Sheets** for nesting pieces onto stock, **Download**, then **Share**. The step list at the top jumps to any step. LightBurn and xTool Studio project files now sit right under **Complete project** instead of inside **Individual files**. Flat engravings open straight on **Download**. See [export files](/guides/export-files).

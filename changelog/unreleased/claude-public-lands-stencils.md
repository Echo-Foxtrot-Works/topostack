type: feature
title: Paint templates for national parks, forests, BLM land and other public lands
---
Layered models in the United States can now get a paper paint template for each kind of public land: national parks and monuments, national forests, wilderness, BLM land, wildlife refuges, state parks, preserves, tribal and military land. Turn them on under **Public land paint** in Map features. Where kinds overlap, the more specific one paints, so templates never overlap. Outlines come from the USGS PAD-US database.

type: improvement
title: A settings rail that shows one panel at a time
---
The studio sidebar is now a rail of seven panels (**Place**, **Terrain**, **Features**, **Water**, **Aviation**, **Labels** and **Fabricate**), so each subject has one home: line widths sit under the feature they draw, and water depth, acrylic inserts and paint templates share the Water panel. **Find a setting** jumps to any control by name. Material-saving nests and seam options moved into the export dialog beside sheet layout.

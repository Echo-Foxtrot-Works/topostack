---
type: feature
title: Build airspace over your model in acrylic
---
Turn on Airspace in 3D in Fabrication settings to build the Class B, Class C and special use airspace over a US model in acrylic, at true height on the model's own vertical scale: as clear plates, tinted wedding-cake tiers, or solid volumes, with optional Class D lids. Rods you cut to length hold each piece, standing in sockets cut into the terrain; the export adds the acrylic panels, a backing sheet, and a rod cut list with a map of every level in the assembly guide. Airspace comes from FAA data and is not for navigation.

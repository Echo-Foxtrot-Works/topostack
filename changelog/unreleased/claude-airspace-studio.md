---
type: feature
title: Build airspace over your model in acrylic
---
Turn on Airspace in 3D in Fabrication settings to build the Class B, Class C and special use airspace over a US model in acrylic, at true height on the model's own vertical scale: as clear plates, tinted wedding-cake tiers, or solid volumes, with optional Class D lids. Rods you cut to length stand in sockets cut into the terrain, as short segments glued between levels or one rod rising through holes in the pieces; the export adds the acrylic panels, a backing sheet, a rod cut list and a map of every level. Airspace comes from FAA data and is not for navigation.

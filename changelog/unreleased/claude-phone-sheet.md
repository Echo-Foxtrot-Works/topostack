type: improvement
title: Edit settings on a phone without losing sight of the model
---
On a phone held upright, the studio now fills the screen: the model stays in view, **Generate terrain** and the panel tabs sit at the bottom, and tapping a tab opens its settings in a sheet below the model. Drag or tap the sheet's handle to make it taller, and tap the open tab, the close button or Escape to put it away.

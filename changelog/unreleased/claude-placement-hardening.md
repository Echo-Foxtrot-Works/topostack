---
type: fix
title: Assembly marks stay hidden under the layer above
---
Layer ids and alignment outlines now keep clear of the edge of the piece glued over them, measured from the engraved line itself, so large text, typefaces with tall figures, wide lines or a zero kerf no longer let one peek out. Ids also avoid seams in the layer above, and elevation labels are no longer engraved on the ledge an acrylic water insert is glued to.

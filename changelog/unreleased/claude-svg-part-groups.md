---
type: improvement
title: Each piece moves as one in xTool Studio and LightBurn
---
Exported SVGs are now grouped piece by piece: every cut piece is one group holding its outline, holes, engraving, score lines and assembly id. Select a piece in xTool Studio or LightBurn and its engraving moves and rotates with it, while the pieces around it stay put. Colours and sizes are unchanged. If you relied on the old per-layer `CUT`, `SCORE` and `ENGRAVE` groups, select by colour instead.

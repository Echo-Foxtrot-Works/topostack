type: feature
title: Engrave public land boundaries and names on any US model
---
Turn on **Public land boundaries** in Map features to engrave national parks, forests, wilderness, BLM land and other public lands, each kind in its own dash, on layered models and flat engravings alike. Add the names of the largest units, and on a flat engraving hatch the inside edge so the land side reads at a glance.

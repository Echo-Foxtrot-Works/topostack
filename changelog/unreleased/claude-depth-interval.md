---
type: feature
title: Fit sea depth to the depth layers you choose
---
A shallow bay no longer has to cut as one flat floor. In **Water**, turn on **Limit depth layers**, pick a count, then **Fit sea depth to these layers**: the sea's depth is spread across those layers, so a bay a few metres deep shows its channels and shoals, and a deep shelf fits in a few sheets instead of twenty. The land is cut exactly as before. Depth labels still read the real depth. The preview says when a sea is too shallow to show and offers the fit in one click; agents set it with `seaDepthLayers`.

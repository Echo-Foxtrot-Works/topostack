---
type: feature
title: Engrave airspace, airports and runways from FAA data
---
In the United States, **Aviation (US)** under Map details adds what a VFR sectional shows: Class B, C and D airspace with its altitudes, special use airspace below 18,000 ft, runways, airports, navaids, obstacles and their identifiers, each in its own SVG group. Shared airspace edges are engraved once. The data is the FAA's own and is decorative only, never for navigation; exports name the cycle it came from.

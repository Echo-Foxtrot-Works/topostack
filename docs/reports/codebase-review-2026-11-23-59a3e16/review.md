## Fix first

1. **P1 · Opening Studio after a transient storage failure can erase the last saved project.** With a valid design in IndexedDB and no outstanding pagehide copy, a failed startup read loads the default. Autosave writes that default over the prior design without any user edit or backup. One reviewer injected a read error. A second aborted an actual Chromium read transaction and observed the next write succeed. Distinguish an empty store from a failed read. Preserve the primary key until recovery and test a one-time read failure followed by successful writes and a reload. High confidence. [Storage read](../../../apps/generator/src/lib/storage/storage.ts#L43) · [Startup autosave](../../../apps/generator/src/lib/studio/App.svelte#L500)

2. **P1 · A valid airspace project can export solid pieces where its assembly guide requires rod holes.** With a 5 mm minimum feature, 4 mm rods and 0.1 mm clearance per side, the model still specified all 13 wood sockets and eight acrylic through-holes. The exported wood and clear acrylic omitted them, with no warning or export block. A second reviewer located column C3 at (−128, −48) mm in the manifest and in both cut files. Both files had a 4.050 mm kerf-compensated hole at the default 0.8 mm setting and no hole at 5 mm. Preserve functional holes through feature filtering, then assert that every guide instruction has a matching final cut. High confidence. [Acrylic hole construction](../../../packages/core/src/pipeline/airspace-supports.ts#L520) · [Wood sockets](../../../packages/core/src/pipeline/airspace-supports.ts#L576)
3. **P1 · A reviewed lake can lose most of its depth before fabrication.** In the Studio chart editor, a valid chart with distinct 5 m and 75 m lines passed registration and reported full contour coverage. Cells touched by both contours were averaged. A second reviewer used a 2,000 px chart with integer vertices four pixels apart and the normal 5 m grid. The deepest sampled water was 39.801 m and the center was 5.012 m. Separating the lines by 12 px restored the 75 m bed. Validate depth constraints in the raster that the lake sampler consumes. Refine the grid or reject the chart if those constraints cannot be met. Retain both spacings as regression controls. High confidence. [Grid constraints](../../../packages/chart-trace/src/grid.ts#L343) · [Studio acceptance](../../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/chart-build.ts#L208)
4. **P1 · A single map can contain road data from two releases.** When the studio reuses an archive reader and one of two concurrent tile reads encounters a new ETag, a pending old tile can still succeed while the second read retries on the new archive. The serving reviewer measured both byte streams. The geographic-data reviewer independently loaded adjacent same-zoom MVT tiles through the production vector loader and received road labels from both releases with no truncation warning. Make publication of results generation-specific, or fail and retry the complete source operation. Test both completion orders with a warm reader. High confidence. [Archive reader](../../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/archive.ts#L127) · [Concurrent vector loader](../../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/vector-loader.ts#L227)

5. **P1 · Rebuilding the lake dataset silently replaces a valid local PMTiles archive with SQLite.** The documented HydroLAKES/GLOBathy build reports success. Its temporary filename ends in `.part`, causing tippecanoe to select MBTiles. The helper then renames the result over the requested `.pmtiles` file. Two independent one-lake shapefile/CSV fixtures reproduced this through the CLI. One started with a verified PMTiles archive, checked its replacement header and ran the normal provision verifier, which rejected the new file before upload. Preserve the format extension in a unique temporary filename and verify it before replacement. Regress a real build over a known-good file. High confidence. [Writer](../../../scripts/lib/lake-archive.mjs#L33) · [Build entry point](../../../scripts/provision/build-lake-data.mjs#L111)

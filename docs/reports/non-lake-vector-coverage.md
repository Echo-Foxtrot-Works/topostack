# Non-lake vector coverage

**Production check, November 22, 2026.** Every country and territory within Web Mercator can request the same Protomaps snapshot. The US and Canada also have named public-land units. The FAA supplies a dated aviation overlay for the US and selected territories. A bounding-box match identifies an archive worth querying. Only a matching feature in the selected crop can produce a mark. [Production manifest](https://topostack.app/v1/manifest) · [Production readiness](https://topostack.app/ready)

## What makes it onto the model

| Layer | Geographic reach | Detail the studio can use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roads, trails and rail | Protomaps worldwide, snapshot 2026-09-05, z0–12 | Major and local roads, paths and surface or eligible covered rail. Ferries, piers, aerialways and aeroways are excluded. Ordinary underground rail and service-yard tracks are filtered. Road, trail and rail names depend on the properties supplied. Romanized alternatives are used for some scripts when present. [Vector loader](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/vector-loader.ts) · [Rail selection](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/covered-rail.ts) |
| Waterways and coastlines | Same archive | The studio engraves mapped water lines and builds shorelines from water polygons. An ocean polygon supplies a water mask. Seafloor depth requires a separate raster. [Vector loader](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/vector-loader.ts) |
| Administrative boundaries | Same archive | Only first-level administrative lines classified as `region` are accepted. An international boundary, county line or municipal outline visible on the reference map can therefore be absent from the piece. [Vector loader](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/vector-loader.ts) · [Protomaps layer definitions](https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/layers) |
| Buildings | Same archive | The studio enables building outlines at **project zoom 13 and closer**. The served archive still stops at z12. The upstream format merges buildings at these zooms. Only `kind=building` polygons that survive crop and minimum-width filtering produce outlines. Feature counts can represent multipart groups of footprints. [Source requirements](../../packages/core/src/pipeline/source-requirements.ts) · [Footprint filtering](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/building-outlines.ts) · [Protomaps building model](https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/layers#buildings) |
| Named public land | US and its territories, Canada | PAD-US 4.1 supplies ten project classes. CPCAD 2025-12-31 supplies selected terrestrial parks, wilderness, refuges, provincial/territorial parks and preserves. First Nations reserves and marine designations are outside the CPCAD selection. The published archive holds 34,634 US and 9,563 Canadian units and serves z4–12. [Build and inclusion rules](../public-lands.md) · [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-data-overview) · [Environment and Climate Change Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/protected-conserved-areas-database.html) |
| Other public land | Mapped places worldwide | OSM contributes three unnamed classes. National parks come from `national_park`, preserves from `nature_reserve` or `protected_area`, and military land from `military`. Other designations have no OSM equivalent in the loader. A local forest polygon, for example, provides no evidence of public ownership. [Land loader](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/lands-provider.ts) |
| Aviation | US FAA archive, including PR, VI, GU, MP, AS and parts of UM | Airports, runways, selected navaids and obstacles, Class B/C/D airspace, and selected special-use areas. Engraving spans z5–12, volumetric sectors z5–10. Airport and obstacle inputs are filtered to US records. Country borders can cut through actual airspace. Production served the Oct 1 NASR cycle. FAA listed Oct 29 as current on the audit date. A dated model is for decoration. [Archive and selection rules](../faa-aviation.md) · [FAA cycle listing](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/aero_data/NASR_Subscription/) |

The production OSM archive was 17,562,176,068 bytes. The header and metadata exposed nine layers, including `places`, `pois`, `landcover` and `earth`. Those four layers do not become general-purpose engraving layers. Station POIs can provide evidence for covered rail. Map display and place search use separate services. The geometry pipeline can also choose coarser tiles for a large or complex crop, drop features below the minimum physical size, and limit markings to 1,800. Local coverage therefore depends on mapping, zoom, crop and model scale. ## Gaps found in the sweep

- **The Bahamas and Mexico have drawable polygons hidden by the production routing check.** Lucayan National Park (1155/1733) and El Pinacate (756/1666) each contained an OSM nature-reserve polygon and no official land feature. The US rectangles cover both entire tiles, so the deployed loader skipped OSM. The Bahamas coverage response also advertised FAA there despite an empty FAA tile. The working-tree fix tries OSM after a complete official search with zero requested units. Selecting preserves makes these two mapped reserves eligible. A partial or failed official read keeps its own status. [Lucayan coverage response](https://topostack.app/v1/coverage?lat=26.61&lon=-78.4&widthKm=5) · [Land loader and regression tests](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/lands-provider.test.ts)
- **A rectangle can also miss data already in storage.** The production Palmyra response listed no public-land source, despite the PAD-US refuge. Baker (40/2045), Howland (38/2038), Jarvis (227/2052), Johnston (119/1854), Kingman (200/1975) and Navassa (1194/1834) had the same problem. The revised registration includes the seven missing UM islands, expands the Midway and Wake boxes to the archived refuge bounds, and covers eight groups of polygons in the Hawaiian Islands National Wildlife Refuge. Those bounds were measured from the named features at z10 in the pinned v2 lands archive. The change reuses the existing data. [Palmyra production response](https://topostack.app/v1/coverage?lat=5.88&lon=-162.08&widthKm=5) · [Revised land registration](../../scripts/data/public-lands-sources.json) · [US Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/locations/hawaiian-islands-national-wildlife-refuge)
- **Other territory overlaps affect coverage claims.** The Canada box includes Sisimiut, Greenland (1437/1011) and Grand Colombier, Saint Pierre and Miquelon (1408/1443). The Puerto Rico/USVI box includes Beef Island airport in the British Virgin Islands (1313/1834). Alaska’s box reaches Provideniya, Russia (77/1081). These foreign test tiles returned no official land or FAA features. The Tijuana airport tile (717/1656) did contain an FAA airspace feature and a navaid, with no FAA airport. A source label for a neighboring country cannot describe all layers at a site. [Land registration](../../scripts/data/public-lands-sources.json) · [FAA registration](../../scripts/data/faa-aviation-sources.json)
- **HFS was present in the FAA archive and outside the registered area.** Production FAA tile 156/1768 contained the private airport `HFS` at 23.864058° N, 166.284642° W. The pinned NASR airport row gives 23.86405° N, 166.28463333° W. The OSM tile places the Tern Island coastline between 23.867727° and 23.872044° N. The revised FAA box encloses the archive point and island footprint, rounded outward to 0.001°. The studio reads the airport symbol from the existing archive. The tile has no runway line. Its decompressed MVT SHA-256 is `7e0d9f2d1c0577b12032f40c95d5a0971c3544b99ce72c0e060a42d5737c74af`. [FAA registration](../../scripts/data/faa-aviation-sources.json) · [FAA source and geometry](../faa-aviation.md)
- **Mixed official/OSM land crops still need a spatial boundary.** A cross-border crop with a matching official unit retains the official result for the entire crop. Supplementing it would require a suitable jurisdiction footprint to avoid duplicate park/preserve stencils. [Land loader](../../apps/generator/src/lib/domain/lands-provider.ts)
